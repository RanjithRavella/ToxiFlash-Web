Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

Welcome to ToxiFlash ("App", "we", "us", or "our").

ToxiFlash is an educational application designed primarily for medical students and healthcare learners to study and revise toxicology.

This Privacy Policy explains how ToxiFlash collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application and related services.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, and improve ToxiFlash.

By using ToxiFlash, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, ToxiFlash may collect the following categories of information.

A. Account Information

If ToxiFlash provides user accounts, we may collect information such as:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number, if required.
  • Authentication information.
  • Profile information voluntarily provided by the user.

B. Search History

ToxiFlash may store information relating to searches performed by users.

This may include:

  • Search terms.
  • Toxicological syndrome searches.
  • Symptoms or clinical information entered into the search field.
  • Date and time of searches.
  • Interactions with search results.

Search history may be stored so users can view previous searches and quickly access previously searched toxicological topics.

C. Educational Activity

Depending on the features available, we may collect information relating to your use of educational features, such as:

  • Syndrome library activity.
  • Toxicology notes accessed.
  • Videos viewed.
  • Live conference participation.
  • Bookmarked or saved educational content.
  • Other interactions with educational features.

D. Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information, depending on the services used by the App, including:

  • Device type.
  • Operating system.
  • App version.
  • Language settings.
  • IP address, where applicable.
  • Crash reports.
  • Diagnostic information.
  • General usage information.

2. Information We Do Not Want You to Submit

Important: ToxiFlash is an educational application and is not intended to be used as a patient medical-record system.

Users should not enter identifiable or confidential patient information into ToxiFlash.

Please do not enter:

  • Patient names.
  • Addresses.
  • Phone numbers.
  • Medical record numbers.
  • Government identification numbers.
  • Identifiable photographs.
  • Hospital identification numbers.
  • Other information that can directly identify a patient.

If you are studying a clinical case, use de-identified information whenever possible.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate ToxiFlash.
  • Provide toxicology syndrome search functionality.
  • Save and display search history.
  • Provide the toxicological syndrome library.
  • Provide toxicology notes and educational resources.
  • Provide toxin and antidote information.
  • Provide educational videos and conference features.
  • Improve App performance and functionality.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Maintain security.
  • Prevent misuse or abuse.
  • Respond to user requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Search History

Search history is an important feature of ToxiFlash.

When you use the search functionality, your searches may be stored so that you can access them later.

Because search terms may contain clinical information entered by the user, we strongly recommend that users do not enter identifiable patient information.

Where available, users may delete their search history using the controls provided within the App.
