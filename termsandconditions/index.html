4. Toxidrome Identification

ToxiFlash allows users to select symptoms, findings, or other clinical features and compares them against the toxidrome information stored in the App. The App then displays the closest matching toxidromes, ranked by a match percentage.

This comparison is performed by automated matching. The match percentage only shows how closely your selections overlap with the stored educational information. It is not a measure of clinical probability.

Results are intended to support learning and educational revision.

A result does not constitute a medical diagnosis and should not be interpreted as confirmation that a person has a particular toxicological syndrome.

Results may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. Users are responsible for independently verifying information before relying upon it.
